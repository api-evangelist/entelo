# Entelo

> Entelo is an AI-powered talent-sourcing and recruiting-automation platform for finding, engaging, and hiring candidates. Entelo was acquired and is now delivered as "Entelo Recruit" within Rival (rival-hr.com), an AI-powered HR orchestration suite spanning recruiting, workflow, learning, and performance. The former entelo.com domain now redirects to Rival. No public developer portal, API reference, or OpenAPI is currently published.
